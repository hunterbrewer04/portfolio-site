Clean TV replaces the ad-filled Google TV home screen with a calm one: a clock and greeting over a grid of the apps you actually use. It comes with a set of simple, undoable scripts that switch off the apps the TV came preloaded with.

Every screenshot here is real, pulled from a TCL Google TV. The app is free to download from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/hunterbrewer04/clean-tv/releases/latest).

## What it does

- A home screen with no ads, no tracking, and no internet access of its own.
- Your apps, in the order you choose, plus quick access to the TV's inputs and settings.
- A clock screensaver that slowly drifts so it can't burn into the screen.

## Notes

- Tiny and light: the whole app is about 115 KB and uses roughly half the memory of the stock home screen.
- Everything is reversible. The scripts preview their changes first, and one command puts the TV back the way it was.
- Streaming logins, casting, and remotes all keep working.
