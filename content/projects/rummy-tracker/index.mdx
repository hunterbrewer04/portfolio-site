Rummy Tracker replaces the paper score sheet for two-player rummy. Start a game with two names and a target score, add each hand as you play, and the app keeps the totals, calls the winner, and remembers every game for a running rivalry.

## What it does

- Quick score entry for each hand, with any hand editable later.
- A running total and progress bar for each player as they race to the target.
- A stats screen with the head-to-head record, win streaks, and best scores.

## Notes

- A native iPhone app built with SwiftUI.
- The scoring rules live in one small, tested piece of the app, so they're easy to trust and easy to change.
- A little celebration when someone wins, with a haptic tap and confetti.
