Apple Calendar MCP lets an AI assistant like Claude read and update my Apple Calendar. It's one small Mac app that talks to the calendar directly, so answers come back almost instantly, Calendar.app never has to open, and nothing leaves my machine.

## What it does

- Answers questions like "what's on my calendar this week?" across all my calendars or just one.
- Creates, moves, and deletes events from a chat with an AI assistant, or straight from the terminal.
- Works on the same Mac, or from another of my machines over my private network.

## Notes

- Every device that connects gets its own key, and any key can be turned off at any time.
- It refuses to run on the network without a key set, so it can't be left open by accident.
- Installs with a single Homebrew command.
