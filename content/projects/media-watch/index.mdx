Media Watch lets an AI agent actually watch a video. Paste in a reel, a TikTok, a YouTube Short, or a local file, ask a question, and it comes back with a short report of what happens, what's on screen, and what's said, with timestamps.

<FlowDiagram id="media-watch-pipeline" />

## What it does

- Downloads the video and shrinks it just enough to send in one go.
- Has a video-capable AI model watch the whole clip, sound included, instead of a few still frames.
- Returns a structured, timestamped summary the agent can reason about, plus an answer to your question.

## Notes

- Works three ways: as a plugin for my agent, as a command-line tool, and as a small Python library.
- Keeps track of exactly what each call cost, so I always know what I've spent.
- Treats anything said in a video as information, never as instructions for the agent to follow.
